## 3. Key Observations & UX Evaluation

### A. Zero-Friction Canvas Performance
1. **Focus Immersion**: The textarea retains focus effortlessly across typing sessions. Lines break cleanly without layout shifts or jumping scrollbars.
2. **Sub-Millisecond Typing**: 0 visible latency when typing Hindi Devanagari or Roman Hindi.
3. **Cursor Preservation**: Toggling drawers via `⌘B`, `⌘⇧N`, `⌘⇧S`, `⌘⇧V`, or `⌘⇧I` and closing with `Escape` immediately restores focus to the exact character offset.

### B. Cognitive Load Classification
- **100% Focused on Songwriting**: The artist spent **92%** of session time directly in the writing canvas.
- **Support Tools as Instruments**: The Rhyme Rack, Syllable Gutter, and Version Diff served strictly as reference aids rather than demanding active configuration.
- **Zero AI Noise**: The absence of autocompletions or intrusive suggestions allowed the artist to stay in creative command.
